## Cross-cutting themes
- The Fed is the common denominator: rate differentials for FX, discount rates and real yields for indices and gold.
- Oil and energy transmit to all five: DAX costs, eurozone/UK trade deficits, US inflation, gold via real yields.
- Geopolitics adds volatility; its effect on gold depends on whether it reaches inflation, policy and the dollar.
- Correlations are regime-dependent. Source: https://rpc.cfainstitute.org/blogs/enterprising-investor/2026/why-yesterdays-market-rules-dont-always-apply-today
